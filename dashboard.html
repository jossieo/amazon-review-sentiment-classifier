A balanced sample of 150 reviews (50 per class) was scored by an LLM (DeepSeek-V4-Flash via an OpenAI-compatible endpoint) on title and text only. Classes: 4–5★ POSITIVE, 3★ NEUTRAL, 1–2★ NEGATIVE. The star rating is used only afterwards to check the model.

The model agrees with the star rating on 112 of 150 reviews (74.7%), missing 38 — but most of the misses are the model reading 3-star reviews as negative: only 16 of 50 true-neutral reviews were kept neutral.
The rating is the referee: 4–5★ = positive, 3★ = neutral, 1–2★ = negative — but the model never sees it.

The 3-star problem in numbers: of 50 true-NEUTRAL reviews, the model kept 16 as NEUTRAL but pulled 34 into another class — most (29) into NEGATIVE and 5 into POSITIVE. 3-star text tends to be terse and complaint-flavoured, so the words read negative.
